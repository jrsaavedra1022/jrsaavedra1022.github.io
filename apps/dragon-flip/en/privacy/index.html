Privacy policy

This policy explains how Jose Roberto Saavedra Carvajal (Jose Saavedra), creator of Dragon Flip, handles information relating to the game, this website and support.

Last updated: September 27, 2026.

The current game runs without ads, accounts, third-party analytics or developer-operated servers. Progress is saved locally. If you contact us, we do receive the information you include in your message.
GamePurchasesSupportYour choices

1. Game information

Dragon Flip saves experience, best score, accumulated points, equipped cosmetics and preferences for language, music, sound effects and steady lighting on your device. These records remember your progress and settings; the game does not send them to developer-operated servers.

The current version has no account registration, online leaderboards, Game Center integration, advertisements or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not request your name, email, contacts, location, camera or microphone to play.

Rewind history and other temporary states are kept during a run. Device backups managed by the operating system depend on your settings and the provider’s policies.

2. Creator support and purchases

The optional Free Spirit pack adds 26 cosmetics without gameplay advantages through a one-time purchase integrated with the App Store or Google Play. This integration is available in test builds; public launch is still being prepared. Apple or Google manages the store account, authorization and billing. The game and this website do not receive card numbers or store passwords.

The app queries the store for the product, price and entitlement, and receives purchase information such as the product identifier, status and transaction identifiers or receipts. It uses this information to verify, activate and restore the pack. On Android it stores the signed receipt and purchase token locally and acknowledges the purchase with Google Play. On iOS it uses entitlements verified by StoreKit. We do not send receipts to developer-operated servers or use them for advertising or analytics.

Restoration queries the same store and requires the account used to purchase the pack. It does not transfer purchases between Apple and Google or restore local game progress. Entitlement may change if the store revokes a purchase. Apple and Google handle their billing and transaction records under their own privacy policies: Apple and Google. Deleting the app does not delete store purchase records.

3. Support messages

If you email popora.support@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the content you send, such as a problem description, device model, operating system and game versions, and attachments. We use this information to respond, investigate bugs and handle your request.

Do not send passwords, card numbers or information that is not needed for your case. Email is handled through Gmail under Google’s privacy policy.

4. Website visits

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics tools, advertising, tracking cookies or registration forms. GitHub may process technical request data, such as IP addresses and browser information, to provide and secure hosting under its privacy statement.

When you follow an external link, the destination service’s policies apply.

5. Use, retention and protection

We do not sell personal information or use it for personalized advertising. Game data remains on your device while the app’s storage is retained. Support messages are retained for as long as needed to handle the request, resolve related issues and meet applicable legal obligations, and deleted when no longer needed.

We limit the use of messages to support and take reasonable measures to protect access. No storage or communication method offers absolute security. Email and hosting providers may process information in other countries under their own policies.

6. Your choices and requests

You can change game preferences in its settings menu. To remove local records, use your device’s storage controls or delete the app and its data. Uninstalling, offloading an app or restoring a backup may have different effects depending on the system; review your backups too. We cannot recover deleted local progress.

To request access, correction or deletion of messages you sent us, email popora.support@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights, such as objection, restriction or a complaint to a competent authority. We handle requests under applicable law.

7. Children

The game does not need personal information to play. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child sent personal information by email, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. Purchases are subject to store parental controls and authorizations.

8. Changes and contact

We will publish policy changes at this URL and update the date. Future integrations that change data handling will be reflected before they are enabled.

Android · Closed test

Help Dragon Flip take flight

Join the Android closed test. A little dragon, portals, meteors and your honest feedback.

Current status

Registration is open. The closed release is still awaiting submission to Google review; installation will be available after approval. Joining the group alone does not start your 14-day test period.

1. Join the group

Use the Google account you use in the Play Store. Choose “Join group”. You do not need to publish your email on Reddit or Discord.

Join Dragon Flip Testers

2. Accept the Google Play test

With the same account, select “Become a tester”. This is a separate step from joining the group.

Become a tester

3. Install and play

Once the closed release is available, install from Google Play. No APK downloads, ads or purchase are required to participate.

Open Google Play

What would help?

Stay opted in to the Google Play test for at least 14 consecutive days. Play on several days and try the wardrobe, portals, sound and touch controls. Tell us what feels good and what needs fixing; no rating or positive review is required.

There is no fixed daily playtime requirement. We need real use and honest feedback, not just group memberships.

Where is the test available?

Colombia, Mexico, Argentina, Chile, Peru, Ecuador, Bolivia, Paraguay, Uruguay, Panama, Costa Rica, Guatemala, El Salvador, Honduras, Nicaragua, Dominican Republic, United States, Canada, Australia and New Zealand.

Cannot access the test?

Check that you joined the group and opted in with the same Google Play account, and that your Play country is listed above. If you are already an internal tester, leave the internal test before opting in to the closed test. A pending Google review can also prevent installation.

Feedback and privacy

Email your device model, Android version and the steps to reproduce any issue to popora.support@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or payment information.

Google manages group membership and test enrollment. Group membership and member email visibility are restricted to group administrators. Dragon Flip does not require an in-game account. See our privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap the play area to flap. Avoid walls and fly through the gaps. On a keyboard, use Space or Up; X activates the energy ball and Z rewinds when charged.

How do powers work?

Blue energy targets meteors. Red energy, obtained from a mushroom, can break a wall section. Collect clock fruit and use the rewind button to go back 1.5 seconds. Rewind does not activate automatically.

How do I unlock worlds and accessories?

Experience unlocks the regular collection. Points accumulated across flights reveal the 15 worlds. The wardrobe shows the requirement for each item; cosmetics do not change your dragon’s performance.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device: experience, best score, total points, outfit, language and sound settings. There is no Dragon Flip account or developer-operated cloud sync. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove progress; support does not hold a backup to restore.

Are there ads?

No. Dragon Flip has no advertisements or rewards for watching ads.

Do I have to support the creator?

No. Free Spirit is an optional pack of 26 cosmetics. Flight, powers and regular progression do not depend on buying it. The items only change appearance.

Can I buy the pack now?

The one-time purchase is integrated in test builds. Public launch is still being prepared. Open Wardrobe → Support the creator to see your local price and store confirmation. You can try the cosmetics for one run for free and repeat the preview; purchasing lets you keep them. Do not send money by email or pay to resolve a support issue.

How do I restore the pack?

Use the same account and store used for the purchase. Open Wardrobe → Support the creator → Restore purchases while connected to the internet. You do not need to buy it again. Restoration recovers access to the pack, not deleted scores, experience or other local records. Purchases do not transfer between the App Store and Google Play. If it is missing, check your store account and contact support without sending passwords or banking details.

Can I change sound, language or lighting?

Open the gear menu to choose Español or English, turn music and sound effects on or off, or enable steady lighting for the purple portal.

How do I report a bug?

Email support with your device model, operating system, game version and steps to reproduce the issue. Include a screenshot if useful, without personal information or payment details.

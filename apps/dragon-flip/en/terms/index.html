Use & creator support

Information about the game and its optional creator-support pack. These notes do not replace store terms or limit your legal rights.

Last updated: September 14, 2026.

A game without ads

Dragon Flip is being developed as an ad-free arcade game. Flight, powers and regular collection progress do not depend on buying the support pack.

Free Spirit: support through cosmetics

The pack is designed as an optional one-time purchase adding 26 cosmetics: colors, horns, wings, hats, extras and auras. They change appearance, not physics, scoring or your chances of winning. It is not a subscription or charitable donation: it supports the creator through cosmetic content.

Purchases are not enabled in the development version. You can preview items in the wardrobe. No price has been published yet, and this website does not accept payments.

When purchases become available

The price and content will appear in the app and store confirmation before payment. Purchase, validation, restoration and availability details will be updated when the payment system is connected. Cross-store transfer between Apple and Google is not promised.

Store licenses and terms

For an App Store version, consult the license shown in its listing. Where no custom license is provided, Apple specifies its standard application license agreement. Applicable distribution and purchase terms govern Google Play versions. This page does not change agreements you accepted with a store.

Purchases and refunds

For a future purchase billed by Apple or Google, use the relevant store’s guidance: Apple refunds or Google Play refunds. Eligibility depends on the circumstances and applicable rules; this page neither guarantees nor excludes a refund.

Local progress and help

Progress is stored on your device. Clearing its data may remove progress; there is no developer-held backup. For technical issues or content questions, email popora.support@gmail.com. We will never ask you to pay by email to resolve a support issue.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 15, 2026

1. General Information

This Privacy Policy explains how Jose Saavedra handles information related to the mobile game Popora. The app is available on iPhone and Android.

2. Information We Collect

Popora does not require account registration. Depending on your device and platform, the app and integrated services may process:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as IDFA/AAID) used by advertising partners.
  • Basic app interaction events (for app performance, ad delivery, and analytics, if enabled).
  • Local settings/preferences stored on your device (e.g., sound, music, vibration, dark mode).

3. Advertising and Third Parties

Popora uses third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob, to show ads in non-gameplay screens (such as Home and Settings).

These partners may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies, including advertising identifiers and device information.

Please review Google policies here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. How Data Is Used

  • To provide and maintain app functionality.
  • To remember your in-app preferences locally.
  • To deliver and measure advertising content through third-party SDKs.
  • To improve app stability and user experience.

5. Data Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect data processed by the app. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. User Rights and Choices

Depending on your region, you may have rights related to your personal data, such as access, deletion, or restriction of processing.

You can also manage ad personalization and tracking permissions from your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Popora is not directed to children under the age of 13 and is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

The app may display advertisements provided by third-party services such as Google AdMob, which may process certain device-related or advertising-related information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at popora.support@gmail.com so we can review the situation and take appropriate action if necessary.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

9. Contact

If you have privacy-related questions, contact: popora.support@gmail.com

Frequently asked questions

Where is my clipboard data stored?

SnapClip stores your clipboard history locally on your Mac.

Does SnapClip collect analytics or track me?

No. SnapClip does not track you or send your clipboard data anywhere.

How do I report a bug?

Email us or open a bug report with your macOS version, SnapClip version, steps to reproduce, and expected versus actual behavior. GitHub requires an account to open an issue; email is also available.

Have a feature request?

Suggest a feature or send us an email.

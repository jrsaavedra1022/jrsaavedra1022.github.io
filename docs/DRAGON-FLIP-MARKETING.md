## English

**Name:** Dragon Flip

**Subtitle:** Fly, dodge and make it yours

**Promotional text:** A little dragon, a grand flight. Discover 15 worlds, use powers and find your style. No ads, with an optional cosmetic pack to support the creator.

**Description:**

Find your rhythm and take your dragon a little further.

Dragon Flip brings arcade flight, powers and customization to a fantasy world. Tap to flap, fly through the gaps and beat your best score. Every flight builds progress toward new landscapes and wardrobe options.

• Fly through 15 worlds with changing light.
• Use energy to face hazards.
• Collect clock fruit and rewind to try to correct your flight.
• Earn experience and customize colors, horns, wings and accessories.
• Adjust music, effects and language in English or Spanish.
• Play without ads and keep your progress on your device.

The optional Free Spirit pack is designed to support the creator and add 26 cosmetics. They only change appearance and offer no gameplay advantage. Purchases are not enabled in the development version yet.

Progress is local, with no developer-operated cloud sync. Clearing app data may remove it.
